# The 14-Day Study Plan

## How the plan works

- **About 4 hours per day:** roughly 2.5 hours reading, 1 hour on labs, and 30 minutes on questions and flashcards.
- **Days 1–4** build the foundations everything else depends on.
- **Days 5–7** are all composition, the largest domain (23%).
- **Days 8–12** cover the remaining domains, roughly in proportion to their weight.
- **Days 12–14** each include a full 120-minute mock exam.

If you have less time per day, stretch the plan rather than skipping labs. Labs are where the concepts stick.

## Daily routine

1. **Warm-up (10 min):** review yesterday's flashcards.
2. **Read (about 2.5 h):** chapters for the day. Predict every Python output before reading it.
3. **Labs (about 1 h):** type and run the day's labs.
4. **Practice (about 30 min):** the day's question set, then mark your answers.
5. **Log (5 min):** write scores and weak topics in the tracker below.
