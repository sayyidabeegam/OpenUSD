### About this book

You do not need any OpenUSD experience to start. You do need basic Python: variables, lists, functions, `for` loops, and running a script from a terminal. Everything else is taught from the beginning.

The book works **offline**. Every chapter contains its own explanations, examples, and answers. URLs appear only in "Further reading" lists and in the References section, for readers who want more depth.
